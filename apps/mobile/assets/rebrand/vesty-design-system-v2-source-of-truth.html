Locked Home direction

Source of truth for the redesign: personal portfolio first, pinned club second, Investment Day third, compact club cards last. Real profile photos are the social identity. Deep Vesty blue is the brand anchor; mint is reserved for performance and positive state.

Locked rules
  • Home is personal, not a Club duplicate.
  • Portfolio period control sits below the chart.
  • One pinned club on Home; one club is auto-selected when it is the only club.
  • Pinning happens from the Club screen.
  • No strategy label on the pinned Home card.
  • Stats are horizontal at the bottom of the pinned club card.
  • Your Clubs uses small member-photo stacks, not emojis or stock-cover art.
  • Profile photo → initials fallback. No Bitmoji system.
